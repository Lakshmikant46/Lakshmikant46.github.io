Laxmikanta Sarangi

Ph.D. Candidate in Economics, IIT Roorkee
Statistical Officer, Directorate of Economics & Statistics, Government of Odisha

Firm-level market power, monopsony, and the (mis)allocation of labour in Indian manufacturing.

I am a fifth-year Ph.D. candidate in Economics at IIT Roorkee, where my work sits at the juncture of macroeconomic development, productivity, and firm and labour-market dynamics. My primary research examines the dynamics of firms' labour-market power within and across industries, and I am equally drawn to the (mis)allocation of labour in the Indian manufacturing sector. I am also interested in globalisation, industrial economics, and the production theory underlying empirical industrial organisation. Alongside my doctoral work, I serve as a Statistical Officer at the Directorate of Economics & Statistics, Government of Odisha. I am keen to collaborate on research related to my thesis topic.

Research interests

  • Market power: markups, wage markdowns, and monopsony in the production-function tradition
  • Labour (mis)allocation and productivity in Indian organised manufacturing
  • Empirical industrial organisation and applied production theory
  • Industrial statistics: the Annual Survey of Industries (ASI) and the Index of Industrial Production (IIP)
